Our Story

More Than Just Kape

Good coffee brings people together.

Welcome to Rene's Table

Kape Ni Rene is inspired by the simple Filipino tradition of sitting down, sharing stories, and enjoying a warm cup of coffee.

From strong Barako coffee to creamy modern favorites, every drink is prepared to make you feel right at home.

"Tara, magkape tayo."

Brewed with heart.

We believe that coffee is not only about the beans. It is about the people, conversations, memories, and moments shared over every cup.
